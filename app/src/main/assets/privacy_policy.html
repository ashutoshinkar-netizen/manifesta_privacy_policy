1. Overview and Commitment

At MANIFESTA ("we", "our", or "us"), we value your privacy above all else. MANIFESTA is a mindful affirmation, manifestation tracking, soundscape meditation, and vision board application designed to empower your personal growth journey.

This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, protected, and shared when you install and use our Android mobile application. By using MANIFESTA, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide, personalize, and synchronize your manifestation experience:

A. Account & Profile Information (Optional)

You can use MANIFESTA as a local guest without registering an account. If you choose to create an account or sign in with Google:

  • Name & Email Address: Used solely to authenticate you and manage your account.
  • Firebase Authentication User ID (UID): A unique, pseudonymous identifier generated by Firebase Authentication to isolate and secure your data.
B. User-Generated Content & Manifestation Data

To help you achieve your goals and track your mindfulness journey, the app stores content you explicitly create:

  • Personal manifestation goals, categories, and completion timestamps.
  • Custom affirmations, mantras, and favorited affirmations.
  • Vision board cards, image tags, and journal reflections.
  • Audio ritual completions, streak counts, and mindfulness history.
C. Device Preferences & Audio Settings

App preference configurations such as notification reminder times, voice pitch, speech speed, preferred soundscape, volume levels, and haptic feedback options are stored locally on your device.

D. Diagnostic and Crash Data

We may automatically receive standard technical device diagnostics, Android OS versions, and non-identifying crash logs to diagnose stability issues and improve app performance.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • Core Functionality: To display your personal affirmations, manage your manifestation goals, and log daily mindfulness streaks.
  • AI-Powered Affirmations: When you request AI affirmation generation, your chosen intention or category is processed securely via Google Gemini API to generate inspirational text. Your queries are not used to train public generative models.
  • Cloud Synchronization: If you are signed in, your goals, affirmations, and profile are backed up securely to Google Cloud Firestore so you can restore them whenever you reopen or reinstall the app.
  • Customer Support: To respond to your questions or data requests when you contact us.

  • Google Firebase Authentication: Manages secure user login, password recovery, and session verification.

Zero Data Selling: We do NOT sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information or manifestation entries to third parties, data brokers, or advertisers. We do not display third-party tracking advertisements in MANIFESTA.

5. Data Storage, Isolation & Security

We employ robust industry-standard safeguards to protect your personal information:

  • On-Device Isolation: Local data is stored securely in an isolated Room SQLite database protected by the Android sandbox.
  • Cloud Isolation: Cloud Firestore security rules strictly mandate that only the authenticated user matching the specific Firebase User ID (request.auth.uid == userId) can read or modify their data.
  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between the app and Firebase/Google servers is encrypted using modern TLS (Transport Layer Security).

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You maintain full ownership and control over your personal data:

  • Access & Modify: You can edit or delete any goal, affirmation, or history entry directly in the app at any time.
  • Account Deletion: You may request complete and permanent deletion of your account and all associated cloud data by emailing us at ashutoshinkar@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". Upon request, your account and all Firestore records will be permanently removed within 30 days.
  • Local Cache Clearing: Uninstalling the application or clearing the app storage from Android Settings immediately purges all local data from your device.

7. Children's Privacy

MANIFESTA does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove the information.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory requirements. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy and providing notice within the app.
