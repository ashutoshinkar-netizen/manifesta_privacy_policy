Delete Your Manifesta Account

You can request deletion of your Manifesta account and all associated data at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to ashutoshinkar@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign up.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete my Manifesta account.
  3. We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

Data that is deleted

Your email address, profile information, and all content you created in the app.

Data that may be retained

Purchase records may be kept for the period required by law and accounting rules.
